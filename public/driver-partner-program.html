Step 2 of 5

The Strategy

Position drivers as business development partners. They are not just hired hands. They are the people who bring the client, while your company handles the luxury fleet, insurance, scheduling, payment collection, support, and dispatch.

1

Recruit the right drivers

Focus on chauffeurs, executive drivers, limo drivers, hospitality pros, veterans, security-minded drivers, and any operator who can confidently build relationships. They do not need to own a luxury vehicle to participate.

2

Give each driver a personal link

Every driver gets a branded booking URL and QR code. They share that link with people they already meet in hotels, airports, offices, churches, weddings, conferences, and concierge relationships.

3

Keep the trip scheduled

Only hourly reservations booked at least 48 hours in advance are allowed through this channel. That gives the platform time to assign the right vehicle and keep operations clean.

4

We provide the vehicle

The platform supplies the sedan, SUV, Sprinter, or executive van. The client experiences a fully branded service, while the driver simply arrives, drives, and completes the trip.

Why Drivers Say Yes

Drivers usually struggle with one of two problems: they own a vehicle and carry all the overhead, or they do not own one and cannot access premium clients. This model solves both.

  • No vehicle purchase required.
  • No invoicing or payment chasing.
  • No scheduling headaches.
  • No insurance administration burden.
  • Potential referral income when they bring the relationship.
  • A clear, premium brand to represent in the market.
Best-practice rule:

Make the referred driver the preferred chauffeur for clients they bring in, as long as they stay active and available. If they are unavailable, the platform can still fulfill the trip with another qualified chauffeur.

How It Connects To Dispatch

The recruiting page brings in the lead, but the dispatcher manager turns that lead into a finished trip. Once a client books through a driver's personal link, the booking enters the dispatch workflow so your team can review the trip, assign the right vehicle, watch for conflicts, and keep the operation clean.

A

Driver brings the client

The chauffeur shares their personal booking page. The client books an hourly reservation at least 48 hours in advance.

B

Dispatcher manager receives the booking

Your internal team sees the reservation in dispatch, assigns the vehicle, checks for conflicts, and keeps the booking visible if it needs waitlist attention.

C

Operations stay in one place

The driver does not have to run payments or logistics. The platform routes the booking into the dispatcher workflow and your team keeps control of fulfillment.

D

Trip is assigned and paid

Once dispatch clears the trip, the platform handles the rest and pays the driver after completion.
